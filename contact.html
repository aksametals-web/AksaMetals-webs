Before You Ask

Questions we get every week.

Why is there no price list?

Copper and brass are priced against the daily LME rate. A published price would be wrong within a week, and you would end up paying for the padding a supplier adds to protect themselves. We quote fresh each time instead.

What is the minimum order?

Usually 50–100 kg for standard sizes. Custom widths or unusual tempers need more, because the mill has to be set up for the run. Ask about a trial quantity if you are testing us.

How long does delivery take?

Typically 3–10 working days depending on form, size and quantity. If you have a hard deadline, say so up front and we will tell you honestly whether we can hit it.

Do I get a test certificate?

Yes — with every batch, not on request. Composition and mechanical properties, traceable back to the heat number.

Can you match a drawing?

Send it over. We will confirm whether the grade, temper and tolerance are within what we can hold before you raise a purchase order.

What are the payment terms?

First orders are normally against advance. Credit terms can be discussed once there is a working relationship. Everything on a proper GST invoice.
