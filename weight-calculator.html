How It Works

No magic — just volume times density.

Every figure here comes from the same arithmetic your own estimator would do.

01

Work out the volume

From the shape and your dimensions — a circle is π r² × thickness, a sheet is length × width × thickness, and so on.

02

Multiply by density

Copper is 8.96 g/cm³. Brass runs 8.39–8.53 depending on the zinc content, which is why choosing the right alloy above matters.

03

Multiply by quantity

Per-piece weight times the number of pieces, then converted to grams, kilograms or tonnes automatically.

✱
This is an estimate. Real weight varies slightly with rolling tolerance, edge trim and surface finish — usually well under 2%. For invoicing we weigh the actual consignment. Use this for planning and budgeting, not for final billing.

A worked example

A copper circle, 300 mm diameter, 0.8 mm thick:

π × (30 ÷ 2)² × 0.08 cm
= 56.55 cm³
× 8.96 g/cm³
= 506.7 g per piece

Type those numbers in above and you should see the same figure.

Planning an order?

Once you have the weight, multiply by the day's metal rate for a rough material cost. Copper and brass are quoted per kilogram and the rate moves daily — so for anything you are actually going to buy, ask us for a firm figure.
