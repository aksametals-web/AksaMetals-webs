Surface finish

Mill finish, bright annealed, or polished on request. Say if the surface is visible in the finished product — it changes how we handle and interleave it.

Edge condition

Slit edge as standard. Deburred or rounded edge available where the material feeds an automatic press or is handled by hand.

Packing

Wooden pallets, coil cores, interleaving paper and edge protection — specified to suit the transport distance and the surface requirement.

Working out weight by hand

If you would rather do the arithmetic yourself, it is straightforward:

Weight (kg) =
  Volume (cm³) × Density (g/cm³) ÷ 1000

Circle: π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × thickness

Sheet: length × width × thickness

Tube: π × ((OD÷2)² − (ID÷2)²) × length

Work in centimetres throughout, or convert from millimetres by dividing the final volume by 1000.

Worked example. A copper circle 300 mm diameter × 0.8 mm thick:
π × 15² × 0.08 = 56.55 cm³ → × 8.96 = 506.7 g per piece.
