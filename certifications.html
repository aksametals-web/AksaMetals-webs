Test Certificate

What actually appears on the document.

So you know before you order whether it satisfies your incoming inspection.

  • ✓Heat / batch number — the traceability link back to the melt.
  • ✓Chemical composition — element by element, against the specified grade.
  • ✓Mechanical properties — hardness; tensile and elongation where specified.
  • ✓Dimensions & tolerance — thickness, width, diameter as supplied.
  • ✓Temper condition — annealed, quarter hard, half hard, full hard or spring.
  • ✓Standard referenced — the IS / ASTM / BS EN specification worked to.
  • ✓Quantity & weight — matched to the invoice and the packing list.
