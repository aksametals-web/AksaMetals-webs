Who We Are

A new name, built on old-fashioned habits.

Aksa Metals is a business-to-business copper and brass manufacturer in Bidar, Karnataka. We supply raw material to the people who turn it into something — press shops, utensil makers, electrical fabricators, hardware manufacturers.

We started in 2026, which means we cannot point at decades of history. What we can offer is the thing that gets lost at scale: your order actually gets looked at.

  • ✓Both metals in one place — copper and brass from the same supplier, on the same invoice.
  • ✓Certificate with every batch — composition and mechanical properties, traceable to heat number.
  • ✓Honest dates — we would rather decline an order than miss the delivery you planned around.
