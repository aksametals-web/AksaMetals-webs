RECOMMENDATION
--------------
These are stand-ins so you can judge the layout. Photographs of your own
Bidar plant, your own coils, circles and sheets, and your own team will
convert far better than stock imagery. Buyers can tell the difference.

HOW TO REPLACE
--------------
Drop your photo in this folder using the SAME filename and the site picks
it up automatically. No code changes needed.

Suggested sizes: hero images ~1800px wide, everything else ~1200-1500px wide.
Save as JPEG, quality 80. Keep each file under about 500 KB so pages stay fast.
