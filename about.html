About Aksa Metals

Metal is simple.
Getting it right is not.

Anyone can sell you copper. Fewer can tell you what temper it is, hold the gauge across a whole coil, and put the date in writing.

Why we exist

Because buyers were tired of chasing suppliers.

Ask anyone who buys non-ferrous metal for a living what frustrates them, and you will hear the same three answers. The gauge drifted halfway through the coil. The delivery date moved twice. The test certificate never turned up.

None of those are hard problems. They are the result of a supplier taking on more than it can look after properly, and quietly passing the cost of that down the line to the person running the press.

Aksa Metals started in Bidar in 2026 to do the ordinary things properly. Roll to the gauge that was agreed. Ship on the date that was promised. Put the certificate in the box. It is not a revolutionary business plan — it is just the part most people stop doing once they get busy.

What we operate by

Three words, and what they actually mean.

01

Precision

Gauge held to ± 0.01 mm on strip and checked during the run, not after it. A tolerance you only measure at the end is a tolerance you cannot fix.

02

Integrity

Quotes priced against the day's real metal rate. If a batch comes out wrong, you hear it from us before it ships — not from your own inspector afterwards.

03

Reliability

We would rather turn an order down than accept a date we are not confident of. A supplier who says no occasionally is worth more than one who is always late.

Where we work

Bidar, and why it matters.

Bidar has worked metal for six hundred years. Bidriware — the inlaid black alloy craft the city is named for — is still made here by hand, and the knowledge that comes with that has never really left.

We are an industrial rolling operation, not a craft workshop. But we are staffed from a place where people grew up around metal, and it shows in the attention paid to a surface finish or a burr on an edge.

Karnataka's northern industrial belt also puts us within practical reach of Hyderabad, Solapur, Pune and Bengaluru — which is where most of our material goes.

What we actually sell

We never see the finished thing.

Everything that leaves here is halfway to being something else. A circle becomes a vessel. A strip becomes a contact inside a switchgear panel nobody will ever open. A coil becomes ten thousand identical pressed parts.

That is worth remembering, because it changes what "good" means. Our customer is not judging the metal on its own — they are judging whether their die ran all day without stopping.
