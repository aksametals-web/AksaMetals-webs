At a Glance

A focused operation, doing two metals properly.

Aksa Metals is a business-to-business manufacturer of rolled copper and brass products, operating from Bidar in northern Karnataka. We supply semi-finished material — circles, coils, sheets, strips, plates and foils — to the fabricators, press shops and OEMs who turn it into finished goods.

We deliberately do not spread across a dozen metals. Copper and brass behave very differently under a roll, and knowing both properly is more useful to a buyer than knowing six of them roughly.

Infrastructure

Melting through to slitting under one roof, so nothing sits waiting on an outside job-worker's queue.

Melting & Casting

Induction melting with charge analysis before the furnace is tapped, then casting into slab for rolling.

Rolling Mills

Multi-pass cold rolling to bring slab down to gauge, with reduction planned so the metal is not overworked.

Annealing

Controlled-atmosphere annealing to set temper and relieve work hardening between rolling passes.

Slitting & Cutting

Slitting to width, circle blanking and cut-to-length, with deburring where the application needs it.

Storage and dispatch

Finished material is stored indoors and interleaved where the surface matters, because a scratch picked up in storage is just as expensive as one picked up in the mill.

Dispatch is handled through established transport partners covering Karnataka, Telangana, Maharashtra and beyond, with documentation prepared before the vehicle is loaded rather than after.

Manufacturing Process

From raw metal to a certified dispatch.

Six stages. Something is measured at every one of them.

01

Raw Material Selection

Cathode, ingot and clean process scrap are segregated by grade and analysed before they are allowed into a charge. Contaminated input cannot be corrected later.

02

Melting & Casting

The charge is melted and brought to composition, verified by spectrometer, then cast into slab. The alloy is fixed at this point — everything downstream is shaping.

03

Hot & Cold Rolling

Slab is broken down and then cold rolled in planned reductions. Rolling to gauge in one aggressive pass is faster and produces worse metal.

04

Annealing

Between passes, and finally to set temper. This is what decides whether a circle draws cleanly or splits at the rim on the first press stroke.

05

Finishing & Cutting

Levelling, slitting to width, blanking circles, cutting to length, deburring and surface cleaning to the finish specified on the order.

06

Testing & Dispatch

Composition, hardness and dimensions confirmed, mill test certificate raised, then packed with interleaving and edge protection as required.

Quality Assurance

Checked during the run, not after it.

A batch inspected only at the end tells you what went wrong. A batch measured during the run lets you stop it going wrong. That distinction is the whole of our quality approach.

Every dispatch carries a mill test certificate covering chemical composition and mechanical properties, traceable back to the heat it was cast from.

  • ✓Chemical analysis — spectrometer check on every heat before casting.
  • ✓Dimensional control — thickness and width measured through the run, not sampled at the ends.
  • ✓Mechanical testing — hardness and, where specified, tensile and elongation.
  • ✓Surface inspection — visual check for scratches, oxide, rolled-in marks and edge burr.
  • ✓Traceability — heat number carried through to the certificate in the customer's box.

Environmental Policy

Copper is worth recycling. So we do.

Copper and brass are among the few materials that can be melted and re-rolled indefinitely without losing their properties. Recycled copper takes a fraction of the energy that primary production does — which makes it both the cheaper and the cleaner input.

That is not a marketing position. It is simply how a non-ferrous rolling mill is supposed to work when it is run properly.

  • ✓Closed-loop scrap — our own process trim goes straight back into the furnace charge.
  • ✓Segregated input — grades kept separate so recycled material stays usable rather than degraded.
  • ✓Responsible sourcing — suppliers expected to document where material came from.
  • ✓Controlled emissions — furnace and annealing operated within the applicable pollution control norms.
  • ✓Reduced packaging waste — reusable pallets and returnable coil cores where the customer can accept them.
